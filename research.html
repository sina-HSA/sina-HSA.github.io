Research

Selected research

Four connected threads with one goal: AI and datacenter infrastructure that is more performant, efficient, scalable, and predictable.

Memory systems

Rethinking the GPU memory hierarchy

Repurposing idle GPU core resources as extra last-level cache, filtering shared and non-reusable data to curb LLC thrashing, hybrid 3D memory, off-chip shared memory, and a precharge-free DRAM architecture.

Understanding and taming traffic

Coordination at line rate
